# **SPONSORSHIP_POLICY.md**  
### *Electrum Civic Atlas — Sponsorship Governance Policy*  
### *Version 1.0 (Institutional, Governed, Launch‑Ready)*

---

## **1. Purpose**

The Electrum Sponsorship Policy establishes the rules governing:

- sponsor eligibility  
- placement definitions  
- creative asset requirements  
- political neutrality  
- brand safety  
- accessibility  
- exclusivity  
- duration  
- conduct  
- cancellation and refund rules  
- enforcement and termination  

This policy ensures that Sponsorship supports Electrum’s civic mission without compromising editorial independence, public trust, or ADA compliance.

This document governs all Sponsorship activity on the Electrum Platform.

---

## **2. Scope**

This policy applies to:

- all Sponsors  
- all Sponsorship Campaigns  
- all Creative Assets  
- all Placement Families  
- all Add‑On Modules  
- all Promo Modules  
- all city layers  
- all Electrum interfaces (web, mobile, VR, future apps)

This policy is binding and enforceable through Electrum’s governance process.

---

# **3. Sponsor Eligibility Rules**

Sponsors must meet all eligibility requirements before activation.

### **3.1 Brand Safety Requirements**
Sponsor must pass Electrum’s brand safety review.

Prohibited categories include:

- hate groups  
- extremist organizations  
- discriminatory entities  
- harmful industries (e.g., weapons, surveillance, predatory finance)  
- misinformation actors  
- violent or graphic content  
- adult content  
- political content (see Section 4)  

### **3.2 Good Standing Requirements**
Sponsor must remain in Good Standing:

- alignment with Electrum’s Civic Charter  
- adherence to Electrum’s values  
- no reputational harm  
- no legal or ethical violations  
- no conduct inconsistent with civic integrity  

### **3.3 Creative Asset Compliance**
Creative Assets must:

- be ADA/WCAG compliant  
- include alt text  
- avoid flashing or harmful animations  
- avoid misleading claims  
- avoid political content  
- avoid discriminatory content  
- be right‑cleared  
- meet Electrum’s technical specifications  

### **3.4 Payment Verification**
Payment must be:

- received  
- verified  
- cleared  

before activation.

### **3.5 Placement Completeness**
All required placements must exist before activation.

---

# **4. Political Neutrality Rules**

Electrum is politically neutral but not morally neutral.

### **4.1 Prohibited Political Content**
Sponsors may not promote:

- political candidates  
- political parties  
- ballot measures  
- partisan political campaigns  
- political fundraising  
- political endorsements  

### **4.2 Permitted Civic Content**
Electrum may permit sponsorship from:

- human rights organizations  
- cultural preservation groups  
- civic education organizations  
- nonpartisan civic institutions  

Electrum retains sole discretion to determine political alignment.

---

# **5. Placement Definitions**

Electrum supports governed placement families.

### **5.1 MVP Placement Families**
- Story Promo Module  
- Object Promo Module  
- Timeline Promo Module  

### **5.2 Future Placement Families**
- Homepage Spotlight  
- Map Promo Module  
- Games Promo Module (Quiz, Crossword, All Games)  
- Citywide Promo Module  
- Add‑On Modules  
- Seasonal Modules  
- VR/360° immersive placements  

### **5.3 Placement Rules**
- placements are governed  
- placements may rotate  
- Electrum does not guarantee impressions or clicks  
- Electrum may adjust placement behavior for ADA compliance  
- Electrum may decline or remove Creative Assets  

---

# **6. Exclusivity Rules**

Electrum does not guarantee exclusivity.

### **6.1 Category Exclusivity**
Electrum may offer category exclusivity in future phases.

### **6.2 Citywide Exclusivity**
Citywide placements may require additional brand safety review.

### **6.3 Homepage Exclusivity**
Homepage Spotlight slots are limited and governed.

---

# **7. Promo Module Rules**

Promo Modules are governed promotional placements.

### **7.1 Allowed Promo Modules**
- Discount Codes  
- Performance Score Rewards  
- Leaderboard Rewards  
- Seasonal Promotions  
- Multicontent Promo Modules  
- Map Promo Modules  
- Story Promo Modules  

### **7.2 Prohibited Promo Modules**
- political promotions  
- misleading promotions  
- promotions requiring personal data  
- promotions involving gambling or lotteries  

### **7.3 Prize Limitations**
Rewards must comply with:

- local laws  
- Electrum’s civic guidelines  
- non‑exploitation principles  

---

# **8. Duration Rules**

### **8.1 Allowed Durations**
- 7 days  
- 30 days  
- 90 days (Citywide only)  

### **8.2 Renewal Rules**
- no auto‑renewal  
- renewal requires new Sponsorship purchase  

---

# **9. Creative Restrictions**

Creative Assets may not include:

- hate or harassment  
- extremist content  
- discriminatory content  
- political content  
- graphic or violent imagery  
- misleading claims  
- inaccessible formats  
- flashing animations  
- unsafe VR content  

Electrum may modify assets for accessibility.

---

# **10. Refund & Cancellation Rules**

### **10.1 Refunds Allowed**
Refunds allowed only:

- before activation  
- if Electrum fails to deliver placement  

### **10.2 Refunds Not Allowed**
Refunds not allowed:

- after activation  
- after campaign start  
- after placement display  
- after termination for cause  

### **10.3 Sponsor Cancellation**
Sponsor may cancel:

- before activation → refund  
- after activation → no refund  

---

# **11. Sponsor Conduct Expectations**

Sponsors must:

- act in alignment with Electrum’s civic mission  
- avoid reputational harm  
- avoid discriminatory conduct  
- avoid extremist associations  
- avoid political influence  
- respond promptly to Electrum requests  
- maintain Good Standing  

---

# **12. Enforcement & Termination**

Electrum may terminate:

- for cause  
- for reputational harm  
- for political neutrality violations  
- for content guideline violations  
- for legal reasons  
- for editorial reasons  
- at Electrum’s discretion with notice  

Termination for cause → no refund.

---

# **13. Governance Process**

Electrum may update this policy through:

- internal governance  
- civic charter updates  
- platform safety reviews  
- ADA compliance updates  
- editorial integrity reviews  

Sponsors will be notified of material changes.
